LLS + dual

Compute the dual b of the current LLS a, then draw the canonical line for [a₀; …, a₂ₙ, −1, b₀, …, b₂ₘ].

LLS + reversed dual

Compute the dual b, reverse its coefficient order, and draw the canonical line for [a₀; …, a₂ₙ, −1, b₂ₘ, b₂ₘ₋₁, …, b₀]. Coefficient signs are preserved.

Both actions use the current plotted LLS. Undo restores the previous line.
